Method

How the brief is made

InnerG Pulse reads the same public labor signals as a layoff-and-hiring tracker. Snapshot 11 Nov 2026. Showing a wrong number is worse than showing a dash.

What it counts

Layoffs are named company cuts. News reports cover announcements of about 100 people or more, worldwide. US WARN notices are the official filings a state publishes before a layoff of 50 or more at one site. A notice that matches a news item is not counted twice.

Hiring is jobs open on a company’s own career page, posted within the last 12 months. “New this week” means roles first posted in the last 7 days that are still open. An open job is not a hire.

What a dash means

A headcount is shown only when the sources agree. Otherwise the row says “—”. Weekly totals add only the numbers that are shown, never estimates. This is not the Bureau of Labor Statistics, and it misses the millions of job changes that are never announced.

What this build shows

The 7-day pulse is 6,942 people laid off and 76,044 new open roles. The layoff list is the latest slice of 270 tracked cuts. The hiring list is the week’s biggest adds. The boards list is 3,462 career pages. Every row leaves to the outlet, the WARN filing, or the company’s own board.

InnerG Pulse is not affiliated with layoffsandhires.com. Facts stay linked to the original source.
